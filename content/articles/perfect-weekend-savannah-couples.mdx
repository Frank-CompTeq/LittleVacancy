## Getting There

Savannah is easily accessible from major cities in the Southeast. Most visitors arrive by car, making it a perfect road trip destination from Atlanta (4 hours), Jacksonville (2 hours), or Charleston (2 hours).

The Savannah/Hilton Head International Airport (SAV) also offers direct flights from many US cities if you prefer to fly.

## Where to Stay

For the most romantic experience, we recommend staying in the Historic District. This puts you within walking distance of the famous squares, restaurants, and attractions.

### Luxury Picks

**The Mansion on Forsyth Park** offers old-world charm with modern amenities. Request a room overlooking the park for morning coffee with a view.

**Kehoe House** is an intimate boutique hotel in a restored 1892 mansion, perfect for couples seeking privacy and elegance.

### Boutique Options

**The Bohemian Hotel** on the riverfront combines Southern charm with hip, modern design. The rooftop bar is perfect for sunset cocktails.

## Day 1: Historic District & Romance

### Morning

Start your day with brunch at **Collins Quarter**, a charming café on Bull Street. Their lavender latte has become a Savannah institution.

After brunch, take a leisurely walk through the squares. Start at **Forsyth Park** and work your way north, admiring the architecture and Spanish moss draped over ancient oaks.

### Afternoon

Book a private walking tour of the Historic District. Local guides share fascinating stories about the city's history, architecture, and yes—its ghosts.

Stop for afternoon tea at **The Gryphon Tea Room**, housed in a former pharmacy with original apothecary fixtures.

### Evening

Dress up for dinner at **The Olde Pink House**, one of Savannah's most romantic restaurants. Request a table in the tavern downstairs for an intimate setting.

After dinner, take a carriage ride through the historic streets. The clip-clop of hooves on cobblestones is pure magic.

## Day 2: River Street & Beyond

### Morning

Sleep in and enjoy breakfast at your hotel, or grab pastries from **Back in the Day Bakery** for a picnic in one of the squares.

Head to **River Street** for shopping and people-watching. The converted cotton warehouses now house unique shops and galleries.

### Afternoon

Take a boat tour on the Savannah River for different perspectives of the city. Or rent bikes and explore the quieter neighborhoods beyond the tourist areas.

For lunch, try **Mrs. Wilkes' Dining Room** for an authentic Southern experience—family-style dining at communal tables.

### Evening

End your trip with cocktails at **The Grey**, a James Beard Award-winning restaurant in a restored Greyhound bus station. Even if you don't dine there, the bar serves excellent drinks in a stunning Art Deco setting.

## Tips & Tricks

- **Book restaurants early** — Popular spots fill up fast, especially on weekends
- **Pack comfortable shoes** — You'll be walking on uneven cobblestones and brick paths
- **Bring layers** — Even in mild weather, evenings can be cool
- **Consider a ghost tour** — They're touristy but fun, and a great way to learn local legends
