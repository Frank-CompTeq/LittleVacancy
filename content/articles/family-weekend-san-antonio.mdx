## Why San Antonio for Families?

San Antonio strikes the perfect balance between education and entertainment. Where else can your kids learn Texas history at the Alamo in the morning and splash around at a water park in the afternoon?

The city is also incredibly walkable (at least the downtown area), which means less time in the car and more time exploring.

## Getting There

San Antonio is a 3-hour drive from Houston and 4.5 hours from Dallas—perfect for a road trip. The San Antonio International Airport also has direct flights from most major US cities.

## Where to Stay

### For Families

**Hyatt Regency San Antonio Riverwalk** puts you right on the River Walk with a rooftop pool the kids will love. The location can't be beat for families who want to walk everywhere.

**JW Marriott San Antonio Hill Country Resort** is farther out but offers a lazy river, water slides, and more resort amenities. Great if the kids need pool time.

## Day 1: The Alamo & Downtown

### Morning

Get to **The Alamo** early (it opens at 9am) before the crowds arrive. The mission complex is free to enter, though guided tours cost extra and are worth it for families with older kids.

Take time to walk through the gardens and explain the significance of "Remember the Alamo!" to Texas history.

### Afternoon

Walk over to the **River Walk** for lunch. **Casa Rio** is a family-friendly option right on the water where you can watch the boats go by.

After lunch, take a **river cruise**. The 35-minute narrated tour is the perfect length for kids and covers the history of the River Walk and downtown.

### Evening

Head to **Mi Tierra Café** in Market Square for dinner. The colorful decorations and live mariachi music will enchant kids of all ages. Don't skip the pan dulce from the bakery!

## Day 2: Theme Parks & More

### Option A: SeaWorld San Antonio

If your family loves marine life and roller coasters, spend the day at SeaWorld. Allow 6-8 hours for the full experience.

**Pro tip:** Buy tickets online in advance for significant savings.

### Option B: San Antonio Zoo & Natural Bridge Caverns

For a more relaxed day, visit the **San Antonio Zoo** in the morning (one of the best in Texas), then drive 30 minutes to **Natural Bridge Caverns** for an underground adventure.

### Evening

Return downtown for dinner at **Schilo's Delicatessen**, a San Antonio institution since 1917. Kids love the German comfort food and homemade root beer.

## Day 3: Missions & Markets

### Morning

Visit the **San Antonio Missions National Historical Park**. The missions south of downtown are less crowded than the Alamo and give a better sense of Spanish colonial life.

Mission San José is the largest and most impressive—don't miss the famous "Rose Window."

### Afternoon

End your trip at **The DoSeum**, San Antonio's children's museum. It's hands-on and educational, perfect for burning off energy before the drive home.

## Budget Tips

- **City passes** can save money if you're hitting multiple paid attractions
- **Pack snacks** — attraction food adds up fast with kids
- **Free River Walk** — walking along the river is free and endlessly entertaining
- **Mission trail is free** — all four missions outside the Alamo are free to visit
